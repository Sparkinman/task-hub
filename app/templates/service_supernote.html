Unofficial — Ratta can terminate this at any time
Ratta publishes no API for Supernote at all: no developer portal, no documentation, no agreement of any kind. The addresses this uses were read out of the Supernote Partner app itself, which makes Task Hub a guest here with no standing to complain. Ratta may change, restrict or block them whenever they choose, without notice and without owing anyone an explanation, and the first sign would be a sync that fails.
If that happens, every other service keeps syncing normally and disconnecting this one changes nothing elsewhere. Nothing you have synced already is lost.

This reads the To-Do app built into the tablet
These are the to-dos you see in Supernote's own To-Do app, and in the Supernote Partner app on your phone. It is a different route from reading the tablet over CalDAV with a calendar app installed on it — that one only ever sees what the installed app syncs.
Syncing runs both ways. Tasks you add, edit, tick off or delete in Task Hub reach the tablet, and changes made on the tablet come back. The lists themselves are only read — Task Hub maps to lists you have already made there, and never creates or deletes one.
