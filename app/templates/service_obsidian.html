What this changes

With this off, Task Hub never writes to your vault, and it is not merely a promise: Obsidian’s own client runs in mirror-remote mode, which reverts any local change before it could reach you. Ticking a task off in Todoist will not tick it off in Obsidian.

With it on, Obsidian’s client is switched to bidirectional and Task Hub may change one thing only: whether a task is ticked, and its completion date. It never edits your wording, dates, tags or anything else, never adds a task to a note, and never deletes one.

Each line is patched in place rather than rewritten, then read back from disk and checked; if anything but the tick has changed, the note is put back as it was. It stops after 20 changes in a single pass, because a sync wanting to rewrite more of your vault than that is likelier to be a fault than an intention.

Even so, this edits real notes. Your vault has version history in Obsidian Sync — that is your undo, and it is worth knowing where it is before you turn this on.
