This setup takes about two minutes

You will choose five things:

  • A username and password for this web page.
  • Your timezone, and how you like dates written.
  • A separate username and password for the built-in CalDAV server.
  • The names of your first task list and calendar.
  • How often Task Hub checks your connected services.
i
Connecting services comes afterwards
Nothing here needs an account with Google, Todoist or anyone else yet. Once setup is done, each service has its own page with a complete, step-by-step guide written for someone who has never seen a developer console before.
▲
Keep your data safe
Everything Task Hub stores — your settings, your tasks and the encrypted keys to your connected accounts — lives in a single Docker volume called taskhub-data. Backing that up backs up everything. Running docker compose down -v erases it.
Nothing is saved until you continue.
