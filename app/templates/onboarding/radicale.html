Why a second password?
This one gets typed into your phone, your laptop and any other CalDAV client, so it travels to more places than your web sign-in does. Keeping them separate means a device you later stop trusting can be cut off by changing this password alone, without touching the login that guards your connected accounts.
This appears in your CalDAV address, so keep it short and simple.
