Why {{ minimum }} minutes is the floor
Every service limits how often it will answer. Polling harder than they allow earns HTTP 429 "too many requests" responses, and Task Hub then has to back off and wait — so syncing too often makes your tasks arrive later, not sooner. With several accounts connected, each pass makes a lot of calls, which is why this floor is enforced rather than merely suggested.
